# Run Performance

A coach-facing system that consolidates athletes' Garmin data so training decisions do not depend on screenshots or manual transcription.

## Language

**Training record**:
The consolidated evidence of an athlete's completed training derived from Garmin data.
_Avoid_: Screenshot report, athlete report

**Coach Dashboard**:
The coach-only view of athlete health, training, progress, and session details used to make coaching decisions.
_Avoid_: Athlete portal, admin dashboard

**Athlete status**:
The athlete's current training or health risk indicated by their available data; it is not an operational failure of the system.
_Avoid_: System health, sync health

**System health**:
The operational condition of data collection, storage, backups, and the Coach Dashboard; it is separate from Athlete status.
_Avoid_: Athlete readiness, athlete risk
